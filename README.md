# Econometric Analysis & Forecasting of Remittance Inflows in Nepal (1993–2024)

An end-to-end macroeconomic analytics and time-series forecasting project evaluating the historical growth of remittance inflows in Nepal, their structural impact on Gross Domestic Product (GDP), and out-of-sample predictive modeling through 2028.

 📌 Executive Summary & Key Results

Optimal Model: ARIMA(1,1,1) achieved high accuracy on the 2020–2024 test horizon with a 7.09% MAPE ($0.685B MAE), outperforming the Random Forest Regressor benchmark (19.79% MAPE).
Future Forecast (2025–2028): Remittance inflows are projected to expand steadily from $11.81 Billion in 2025** to $13.43 Billion by 2028.

🛠 Project Workflow & Technical Architecture

1. Data Preprocessing:
   - Filtered out 2025 entry anomalies to analyze a stable 32-year historical timeline (1993–2024).
   - Generated natural log transformations (`log_Remittance`, `log_GDP`, `log_Exchange_rate`, etc.) to measure elasticity coefficients.
   - Computed `Remittance_share_GDP` to assess macroeconomic reliance over time.

2. Econometric Diagnostics:
   - Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) Test: Evaluated unit root stationarity properties on logged remittance series.
   - Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Regression: Quantified multivariate elasticity relationships across GDP, exchange rates, inflation, and trade openness.

3. Predictive Modeling & Out-of-Sample Forecasting:
   - Train/Test Split: Evaluated performance by training on 1993–2019 data and backtesting against 2020–2024 real values.
   - ARIMA(1,1,1):Fitted on the complete dataset to project multi-year out-of-sample growth through 2028.
